High-Precision CNC Tool Holding

Clamping repeatability within 3 microns (≤ 0.003mm TIR). Shrink Fit, Hydraulic Expansion, and Ultra-Precision Collet systems dynamically balanced to G2.5 at 25,000 RPM.

Sub-Micron Spindle Concentricity

Extend Tool Life By Up To 300%

Every 0.01mm of toolholder runout reduces cutting tool life by approximately 10-15% due to uneven flute loading and chatter harmonics. K-Precision tool holding systems are ground on 7:24 taper and HSK face-contact surfaces to AT3 taper accuracy, guaranteeing total radial runout ≤ 0.003mm measured at 3x tool diameter.

Shrink Fit (Thermal)

Inductive Shrink Fit

Ultra-slim 3° and 4.5° nose contours deliver supreme accessibility into deep mold cavities without risking collision. 360° concentric grip with massive torque transmission.

  • Runout: ≤ 0.003 mm at 3xD
  • Balance: G2.5 @ 25,000 RPM
  • BT30, BT40, BT50, HSK-A63, HSK-E40
Hydraulic Expansion

Hydraulic Chucks

Internal pressurized oil membrane absorbs cutting micro-vibrations, yielding exceptional surface finishes and mirror reaming tolerances. Rapid single-wrench tool changes.

  • Runout: ≤ 0.003 mm repeatability
  • Damping ratio: 2.5x higher than mechanical
  • Direct bores: Ø12, Ø20, Ø32mm + reducer sleeves
ER / SK Collets

Ultra-Precision Collets

DIN 6499B ER and high-rigidity SK collet chucks with ball-bearing clamping nuts. Special anti-friction coatings reduce collet twist during tightening.

  • UP grade collets: ≤ 0.005 mm TIR
  • Sizes: ER11, ER16, ER20, ER25, ER32, ER40
  • Internal coolant seal discs up to 70 Bar
