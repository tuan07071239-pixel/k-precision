Solid Carbide Cutting Tools & Inserts

Engineered from ultra-fine 0.4µm sub-micron tungsten carbide rods with multi-layer nano-composite coatings for high-efficiency machining in titanium, superalloys, and hardened steels up to 68 HRC.

Micro-Grain Carbide Substrates

Maximum Edge Retention & Vibration Suppression

K-Precision solid carbide cutting tools combine unequal flute spacing (variable helix 35°/38°) with specialized eccentric relief edge preparations to disrupt harmonic resonance. Whether running trochoidal roughing paths or high-speed finishing passes, our tools maintain h6 shank tolerances to prevent slip inside precision hydraulic and shrink-fit holders.

Surface Engineering

Next-Generation PVD Tool Coatings

Tailored thin-film surface treatments maximizing oxidation resistance and lubricating chip evacuation.

AlCrN Nano-Composite

AlCrN Multi-Layer

Ultra-high thermal barrier withstands oxidation up to 1,100°C. Outstanding wear resistance for high-feed dry/wet milling in alloy steels and cast irons.

TiSiN Ultra-Hard

TiSiN Hard Coat

Silicon-doped nano-crystalline structure providing extreme micro-hardness for hardened mold steels (H13, D2, S7) from 50 to 65 HRC.

Hardness: 4200 HV
DLC Diamond-Like

DLC Non-Ferrous

Ultra-low friction coefficient (μ ≤ 0.08) completely eliminates aluminum adhesion and chip welding in 6061/7075 alloys and plastics.

Coeff: μ < 0.08
TiAlN / AlTiN

AlTiN High-Heat
