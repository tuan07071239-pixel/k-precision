Solid Carbide Cutting Tools & Inserts

Engineered from ultra-fine 0.4µm sub-micron tungsten carbide rods with multi-layer nano-composite coatings for high-efficiency machining in titanium, superalloys, and hardened steels up to 68 HRC.

Micro-Grain Carbide Substrates

Maximum Edge Retention & Vibration Suppression

K-Precision solid carbide cutting tools combine unequal flute spacing (variable helix 35°/38°) with specialized eccentric relief edge preparations to disrupt harmonic resonance. Whether running trochoidal roughing paths or high-speed finishing passes, our tools maintain h6 shank tolerances to prevent slip inside precision hydraulic and shrink-fit holders.
