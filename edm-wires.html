High-Performance EDM Wires & Consumables

Ultra-straight brass and high-speed zinc-coated EDM wires with guaranteed automatic wire threading (AWT) reliability, paired with high-efficiency sub-micron filters, mixed-bed deionization resin, and diamond guides.

Uncompromising Discharge Stability

Zero-Jam Automatic Wire Threading (AWT)

In unattended lights-out Wire Electrical Discharge Machining (WEDM), broken or curling wire causes costly machine downtime. K-Precision brass and coated wires undergo precision in-line continuous annealing to ensure immaculate straightness, clean paraffin-free surfaces, and uniform tensile strength from the first meter to the core of each spool.

Wire Metallurgy

Plain Brass vs. Advanced Zinc-Coated

Select the optimal wire composition for standard precision or accelerated high-speed mold production.

Standard CuZn35 Brass

High-Tensile Brass Wire

Ultra-clean brass wire with 980 N/mm² tensile strength. Formulated for seamless auto-threading in modern submerged CNC wire EDM machines. Zero copper dust flaking.

  • Diameters: Ø0.20, Ø0.25, Ø0.30 mm
  • Spools: P5 (5kg), P10 (10kg), DIN160/200
  • Applications: General mold & die punch machining
Gamma-Phase Zinc Coated

Diffusion Coated Wire

Features a thermally diffused porous zinc-alloy outer layer with lower evaporation temperature, creating stronger flushing gas pressure for 20-30% faster cutting speeds.

  • Cutting speed boost: +25% in tall blocks
  • Prevents wire break on stepped profiles
  • Outstanding surface roughness (Ra < 0.2µm)
Fine & Micro-Wire

Ultra-Fine Tungsten & Brass

Micro-diameter wires designed for precision electronics, lead-frame dies, and medical capillary slots where inner corner radii must remain under 0.05mm.

  • Micro-diameters: Ø0.05, Ø0.07, Ø0.10 mm
  • Extreme tensile strength up to 2200 N/mm²
  • Inner corner radius capability down to R0.03mm
