Precision Superabrasive Grinding Wheels

Engineered Diamond and CBN wheels for CNC tool & cutter grinding, surface/cylindrical finishing, optical glass profiling, and ceramic component machining.

Superabrasive Technology

Maximum Metal Removal Rate, Minimal Thermal Stress

K-Precision grinding wheels utilize synthetic diamond and cubic boron nitride (CBN) crystal grains tightly bonded in tailored vitrified, resinoid, and hybrid metal matrixes. With controlled porosity for superior coolant retention and rapid chip flushing, our wheels prevent workpiece burn and micro-cracking even during deep single-pass creep-feed grinding.

Bond System Architecture

Selecting the Right Bonding Matrix

Each bond system is tuned to balance wheel life, self-sharpening dressability, and heat dissipation.

Vitrified Bond (V)

Vitrified Diamond & CBN

Porous ceramic glass bond offering exceptional rigidity and dressing form retention. Ideal for automated CNC multi-axis tool grinding (PCD/carbide inserts, end mill fluting).

  • Thermal stability up to 1000°C
  • Easily dressed with rotary diamond rolls
  • Open pore structure for high coolant delivery
Resinoid Bond (B)

Phenolic & Polyimide Resin

Slightly elastic bond providing superior surface finishes (Ra < 0.05µm), excellent chatter damping, and high resistance to chipping on sharp cutting edges.

  • Best for wet/dry finish grinding & gashing
  • High vibration absorption capability
  • Fast stock removal without chatter marks
Metal & Electroplated (M)

Sintered Bronze & Nickel Plated

Ultra-durable single-layer or sintered metal bond for profile grinding of optical glass, graphite electrodes, quartz, and heavy roughing of abrasive composites.

  • 100% abrasive grain exposure
  • Complex form profile reproduction
  • Zero wheel dressing required on plated tools
