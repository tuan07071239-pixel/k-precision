Our Heritage & Mission

Enabling High-Precision Manufacturing Excellence

In high-speed 5-axis CNC machining, micro-cavity EDM, and superabrasive tool grinding, your process stability is only as reliable as your weakest consumable part. A toolholder with 0.01mm of runout destroys cutter edges; an inconsistent EDM wire creates micro-arcing and witness lines on mold surfaces; a poorly balanced grinding wheel leaves chatter marks on carbide inserts.

At K-Precision, we engineer, inspect, and stock consumable tooling built to eliminate these variables. By partnering directly with advanced materials synthesizers and maintaining in-house metrology verification, we supply tier-one manufacturers with tools they can trust in unattended night shifts.

In-House Metrology & Inspection

The K-Precision Verification Protocol

Before any batch is packed, it passes through multi-stage coordinate and optical verification.

Stage 01

Raw Material Purity & Metallography

Every incoming carbide rod, toolholder alloy billet, and brass ingot undergoes spectroscopy and grain structure microscopy to ensure zero internal voids or porosity.

  • X-ray fluorescence (XRF) alloy verification
  • Tungsten carbide cobalt binder ratio testing
  • Copper-zinc phase distribution in EDM wire
Stage 02

Sub-Micron Coordinate & Optical CMM

Multi-axis CNC optical vision systems and ruby-stylus coordinate measuring machines verify taper angles, runout, and micro-geometry down to ±0.0008mm.

  • 3D contact CMM for 7:24 & HSK tapers
  • Non-contact laser profile scan for cutting flutes
  • Granite surface plates rated Grade 00
Stage 03

High-Speed Dynamic Balancing & Tension

Spindle toolholders are balanced to ISO 1940-1 Grade G2.5 at 25,000 RPM. EDM wires are tested on continuous pull tensioners to verify break resistance.

  • Laser dynamic balancing machines
  • Tensile strength & elongation curve logging
  • 100% automated spool winding inspection
