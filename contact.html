Ordering & Procurement

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding minimum orders, custom tooling turnaround, and delivery guarantees.

What are the Minimum Order Quantities (MOQs)?

For standard catalog items (solid carbide end mills, shrink fit holders, EDM wire spools, and standard grinding wheels), there is no MOQ—you can order single piece evaluation units. For custom-profiled grinding wheels and custom step tools, typical prototype MOQs are 2-5 pieces.

How quickly do standard consumable items dispatch?

Standard stock orders placed before 3:00 PM EST ship the same business day via UPS, FedEx, or dedicated freight for multi-pallet wire shipments. Standard overnight delivery is available across North America.

Can K-Precision grind custom diamond/CBN wheels according to our machine drawings?

Yes. We regularly engineer custom wheel shapes (1V1, 14A1, 11V9, and custom radius profiles) with specific arbor bores, keyways, and grit concentrations. Typical custom wheel lead times are 2-3 weeks from drawing sign-off.

Do you provide Certificates of Conformity and CMM inspection data?

Yes. Every production lot is tracked under our ISO 9001:2015 quality system. Material test reports, hardness certificates, and coordinate metrology runout sheets can be enclosed with your shipment or emailed directly to your QA department.
