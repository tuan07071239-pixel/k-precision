Our innovative manufacturing technologies

Precision solutions engineered for consistent material removal and fine surface quality.

High-performance diamond and CBN grinding wheels designed for stable cutting action, reduced thermal impact, and dependable surface finish across hard and difficult-to-machine materials.

High-durability tooling designed for cutting accuracy and extended tool life.

Solid carbide end mills, drills, and inserts engineered with wear-resistant coatings, offering smooth chip evacuation and sharp edge retention across diverse CNC milling applications.

Reliable clamping solutions engineered to enhance machining rigidity and minimize vibration.

Thermal shrink-fit and precision hydraulic chucks providing balanced rotational stability and low runout, helping protect spindles and extend cutting tool life.

Reliable discharge wires engineered for smooth threading and consistent cutting accuracy.

High-purity brass and coated EDM wires delivering stable electrical conductivity and clean discharge performance for high-precision wire electrical discharge machining.
