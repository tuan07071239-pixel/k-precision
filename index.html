Sub-Micron Quality Control

Zero Compromise on Tolerances

Every grinding wheel, cutting tool shank, and holder leaving K-Precision is subjected to multi-point optical and coordinate metrology.

Sub-Micron Laser Metrology

Shank concentricity and tool radial runout inspected on granite-damped optical comparators to guarantee repeatability under 0.003mm.

Dynamic High-RPM Balancing

Holders pre-balanced to ISO 1940-1 Grade G2.5 at 25,000 RPM, protecting spindle bearings from high-frequency micro-vibration.

Full Material Traceability & CMM Reports

Certificates of Analysis (COA) and batch test records available with every production shipment upon request.
